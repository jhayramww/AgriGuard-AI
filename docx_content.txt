Project Title

AgriGuard AI: Real-Time Crop Disease Detection & Multilingual Voice Advisory Platform

Problem Statement

Farmers often fail to identify crop diseases at an early stage due to lack of agricultural experts and proper diagnostic tools. Incorrect disease identification leads to excessive pesticide usage, low crop yield, financial losses, and environmental pollution. Most existing systems only detect diseases from images and do not provide multilingual interaction, voice support, fertilizer recommendations, or trusted product purchase options.

The objective of this project is to develop an AI-powered web application that can detect crop diseases in real time using images and provide smart agricultural guidance through text, voice, and multiple languages. The system will recommend suitable fertilizers, pesticides, organic solutions, preventive measures, and trusted online/offline stores for purchasing agricultural products.

Abstract

The Real-Time Crop Disease Detection & Smart Agricultural Advisory System is an AI-powered web application designed to assist farmers in identifying crop diseases quickly and accurately. Users can upload crop images, speak through voice, or enter text queries in their preferred language.

The system uses Deep Learning (CNN/Transfer Learning) to detect crop diseases and Large Language Models (LLMs) for intelligent advisory. Once a disease is detected, the application provides disease information, causes, symptoms, severity level, treatment methods, recommended fertilizers, pesticides, organic alternatives, preventive measures, irrigation suggestions, and nearby/online purchasing options.

The application supports multilingual communication with speech-to-text and text-to-speech technologies, making it easy for farmers from different regions to interact naturally. The project aims to reduce crop losses, improve agricultural productivity, and promote sustainable farming practices.

Introduction

Agriculture contributes significantly to the economy, but crop diseases reduce productivity every year. Farmers often rely on manual inspection or local experts, which may not always be available.

Recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, and Speech Processing enable automated crop disease diagnosis with high accuracy.

Module 13: Smart Weather Forecast & Disease Risk Prediction

Description

The Smart Weather Forecast module automatically detects the farmer's current location (or allows manual location selection) and displays the daily weather forecast. Based on weather conditions such as temperature, humidity, rainfall, and wind speed, the AI predicts the likelihood of crop diseases and provides preventive recommendations.
